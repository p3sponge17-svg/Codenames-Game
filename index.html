How to Play

Setup:

  • Join Red or Blue team
  • One player per team becomes the Spymaster
  • Click "Start Game" to begin

Gameplay:

  • Spymaster gives one-word clues + a number
  • Operatives can vote on up to Clue Number + 1 tiles at once
  • If a team has >2 players, all operatives must agree on a tile to flip it
  • Red has 9 words, Blue has 8
  • AVOID THE HIDDEN ASSASSIN - The Team who finds the assassin loses automatically
