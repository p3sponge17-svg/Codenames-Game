How to Play

Setup:

  • Join Red or Blue team
  • One player per team becomes the Spymaster
  • Click "Start Game" to begin

Gameplay:

  • Spymaster gives one-word clues + a number
  • Operatives discuss then click a word to propose a guess
  • If a team has >2 players, all non-spymaster players on that team must click the same word to confirm the guess
  • Red has 9 words, Blue has 8; avoid the assassin!
